# Indonesian Payroll — Legal Reference (Omnibus Law, UU HPP, PMK 168/2023)

## 1. Income Tax (PPh 21) — TER System 2024

Since January 1, 2024, monthly PPh 21 is calculated using TER (Tarif Efektif Rata-rata).

### TER Categories (based on PTKP status):
- **Category A**: TK/0, TK/1, K/0
- **Category B**: TK/2, TK/3, K/1, K/2
- **Category C**: K/3

### NPWP Rule:
If employee has no NPWP, tax rate increases by 20%.

### PTKP (Non-Taxable Income) — Annual Values:
| Status | Amount (IDR) |
|--------|-------------|
| TK/0 (Single) | 54,000,000 |
| TK/1 (Single + 1) | 58,500,000 |
| TK/2 (Single + 2) | 63,000,000 |
| TK/3 (Single + 3) | 67,500,000 |
| K/0 (Married) | 58,500,000 |
| K/1 (Married + 1) | 63,000,000 |
| K/2 (Married + 2) | 67,500,000 |
| K/3 (Married + 3) | 72,000,000 |

### Progressive Tax Rates (Annual Reconciliation):
| Taxable Income (IDR) | Rate |
|----------------------|------|
| 0 - 60,000,000 | 5% |
| 60,000,001 - 250,000,000 | 15% |
| 250,000,001 - 500,000,000 | 25% |
| 500,000,001 - 5,000,000,000 | 30% |
| > 5,000,000,000 | 35% |

## 2. Social Security (BPJS)

All employers must register employees from day one.

### BPJS Kesehatan (Health):
- Total contribution: 5% of fixed salary (Gaji Pokok + Tunjangan Tetap)
- Employer: 4%
- Employee: 1%
- Cap: Maximum base for calculation is 12,000,000 IDR

### BPJS Ketenagakerjaan (Employment):
| Program | Employer | Employee | Cap |
|---------|----------|----------|-----|
| JHT (Old Age) | 3.7% | 2% | No cap |
| JP (Pension) | 2% | 1% | 10,547,400 IDR (2025) |
| JKK (Work Accident) | 0.24% - 1.74% | - | Risk class dependent |
| JKM (Death) | 0.3% | - | No cap |

### JKK Risk Classes:
| Class | Rate | Examples |
|-------|------|----------|
| Very Low (I) | 0.24% | Office, services |
| Low (II) | 0.54% | Retail, restaurant |
| Medium (III) | 0.89% | Manufacturing |
| High (IV) | 1.27% | Construction |
| Very High (V) | 1.74% | Mining |

## 3. Overtime (Lembur)

Formula: 1/173 of monthly salary per hour.

### Coefficients:
- First hour: 1.5x hourly rate
- Second hour onwards: 2.0x hourly rate

### Limits (UU Cipta Kerja):
- Maximum 4 hours per day
- Maximum 18 hours per week

## 4. THR (Holiday Bonus)

- Must be paid no later than 7 days before the religious holiday
- >= 12 months tenure: 1 full monthly salary
- 1-12 months tenure: (months worked / 12) x salary

### Religious Holidays by Religion:
| Religion | Holiday |
|----------|---------|
| Islam | Idul Fitri |
| Christian/Catholic | Christmas |
| Hindu | Nyepi |
| Buddhist | Waisak |
| Confucian | Imlek |

## 5. Regional Minimum Wage (UMP/UMK)

- 38 provinces with different UMP rates
- 514 kabupaten/kota with specific UMK rates
- Salary cannot be below applicable UMP/UMK
- Updated annually (effective January 1)

## 6. Payment & Reporting Deadlines

| Obligation | Deadline |
|------------|----------|
| BPJS Kesehatan | 10th of current month |
| BPJS Ketenagakerjaan | 15th of following month |
| PPh 21 Payment | 15th of following month |
| PPh 21 Report (SPT Masa) | 20th of following month |

## 7. Data Retention

All payroll records, tax documents, and social security records must be retained for minimum 5 years for audit purposes.
